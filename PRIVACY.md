# Privacy Policy for Weeks

**Effective date:** 8 November 2026

Weeks is a Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with a view of your life in weeks. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where that data goes.

## What Weeks stores

When you use Weeks, it stores:

- your **date of birth**
- your chosen **life expectancy** (in years)
- your **week start day** (Sunday or Monday)
- up to 12 **rules** you write yourself

Weeks uses this data only to draw your grid and show your rules on the new tab page.

## Where it is stored

All data is saved with Chrome's built-in storage (`chrome.storage.sync`) in your own Chrome profile. If you have Chrome Sync turned on, Google syncs it to your other signed-in devices. That sync is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What Weeks does not do

- It does not send your data to the developer or to any server. The extension makes no network requests.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or ads.
- It does not read your browsing history, your tabs or the content of the websites you visit.
- It does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
- It does not use your data for any purpose other than showing it back to you, and never for credit or lending decisions.

## Permissions

Weeks asks for one permission, `storage`, so it can save the data listed above.

## Deleting your data

Open a new tab, click **Settings**, then **Delete all my data** (click twice to confirm). This removes everything Weeks has stored, both in this browser and in Chrome Sync. To make sure nothing is left behind, use **Delete all my data** before you uninstall the extension.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. The full history is in this repository's commit log.
